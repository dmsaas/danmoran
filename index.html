01 / About

Words about
being alive.

I am interested in the things that stay with us: the books we read, the people we lose, the places we remember, and the versions of ourselves that refuse to disappear.

My writing moves between personal experience, fiction, memory and literary reflection. I like work that feels intimate without becoming confessional, strange without losing its human centre, and atmospheric without sacrificing the story.

Above all, I am interested in the emotional residue of things — what remains after an event has finished, a person has gone, or a book has been closed.

02 / Style

Dark places.
Human things.

I am drawn to writing that creates an atmosphere before it explains itself. Stories where place matters, where language has weight, and where something slightly unsettling sits beneath the surface of ordinary life.

Atmosphere

Cities at night. Empty rooms. Rain against glass. The feeling that something has happened — or is about to. Setting is not decoration; it is part of the story.

Mortality

Death, ageing, memory and the awareness that everything eventually disappears. Not as abstract ideas, but as forces operating quietly inside everyday lives.

Memory

The unreliable archive of a human life. What we remember, what we invent, and the strange distance between what actually happened and the story we tell ourselves about it.
